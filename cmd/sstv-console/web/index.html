1. Sound from the radio

The radio's USB sound card. On an FT-710 it shows up as a USB audio device.

Saves what the decoder hears as a WAV file, for troubleshooting.

3. Transmit

Your callsign goes on the pictures you send. The output is the radio's USB sound card, not your speakers.

The test tone plays on the chosen output without keying the radio.
