SSTV Console for the Mac (Apple Silicon)

1. Drag "SSTV Console.app" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS says Apple "could not verify" it. That's because the app isn't registered
   with Apple (it costs $99 a year), not because anything was found wrong. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to "SSTV Console". Enter your password, then click Open.
   (Right-click > Open no longer works on macOS 15 and later.)
4. That's the last time: SSTV Console checks for new versions on its own and offers
   "Install and restart". About > Check for updates checks right away.

Full steps with explanations, how to verify your download, and the source code:
https://github.com/Jnubbz/sstv-console/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md
